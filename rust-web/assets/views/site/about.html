Who it is for

PostDeploy is for developers and small teams who ship their own software and ask a coding agent to help when production breaks.

Your application sends errors and logs with a Sentry SDK. Your CI records each deployment. Your agent then reads one error with its stack, the related logs and the deployment that was live, and reports what it found.

Who runs it

ALPLUS TECHNOLOGIES SRL, a company registered in Romania, provides PostDeploy. One person operates it. Write to hello@alplustech.com.

If you leave

Your SDKs send to a standard Sentry DSN. To move to another service, change the DSN in your configuration.
