What the service is

PostDeploy stores the errors, structured logs and deployment records that your software sends to it. You and the agents you authorize read them through the dashboard, an MCP server and an HTTP API.

The free trial

Every new organization starts with a {{ plan.trial_days }}-day free trial. The trial needs no credit card and includes every feature and allowance of the paid plan.

When the trial ends without a subscription, PostDeploy stops accepting new errors and logs for the organization. Stored data stays readable until its retention period ends.

Subscription

The subscription costs {{ plan.price }} per year per organization, billed annually through Stripe. There is one plan. Stripe processes the payment. PostDeploy does not store card details.

Subscribing during or after the trial keeps the organization, its projects and its stored data.

Allowances

The plan includes {{ plan.errors }} error events and {{ plan.log_volume }} of logs per organization per calendar month (UTC). When an allowance is used up, PostDeploy refuses new data of that type until the next month starts. Going over an allowance adds no charge to your bill.

Organizations and data

Errors are kept {{ plan.error_retention_days }} days and logs {{ plan.log_retention_days }} days from the time PostDeploy receives them. An owner can delete an organization at any time. Deleting it deletes its projects, stored errors, logs and API keys.

Backup sets keep copies of deleted and expired data for {{ plan.backup_retention_days }} days. The first successful backup after that deletes them.
